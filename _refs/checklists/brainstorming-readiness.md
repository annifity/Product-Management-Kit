# Brainstorming Readiness Checklist

- Problem is stated from the user's point of view.
- Primary users and jobs are named.
- Desired outcome is measurable.
- Evidence and confidence are visible when discovery has happened.
- Proposed solution is separated from the problem.
- Scope in and scope out are explicit.
- Constraints are known.
- Assumptions are marked.
- Open questions are visible.
- User has confirmed the brief.
