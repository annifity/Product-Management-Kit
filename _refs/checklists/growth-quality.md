# Growth Quality Checklist

- ICP, product model, natural frequency, critical event, and growth horizon are explicit.
- User, account, logo, and revenue retention are not mixed.
- Rates include denominators, windows, cohorts, segments, and sources.
- The binding constraint is sized against downstream value.
- Correlated behaviors include confounders and a causality test.
- Product-quality failures are checked before messaging or notification tactics.
- Interventions target the constraint and include guardrails, owners, and minimum tests.
- Generic engagement, vanity growth, and unsupported benchmark recommendations are rejected.
- Exactly one PM decision verdict and review trigger are present.
