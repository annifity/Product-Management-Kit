# Finance Metrics Checklist

Use when a product decision needs commercial rigor: pricing, feature investment, growth channel scale, retention, SaaS health, or executive review.

## Metric Families

| Family | Metrics | Use For |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue and Growth | Revenue, MRR, ARR, ARPU, ARPA, churn, NRR, expansion, quick ratio | Momentum, retention, monetization, product-market fit signals. |
| Unit Economics | CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC, payback, gross margin, contribution margin | Whether growth or a feature is economically viable. |
| Capital Efficiency | Burn rate, runway, OpEx, net income, Rule of 40, magic number | Whether growth is sustainable. |
| Portfolio and Concentration | revenue mix, revenue concentration, cohort performance | Business fragility and strategic focus. |

## Decision Frameworks

### Feature Investment

Build when at least one condition is true:

- Year-one ROI is greater than 3x for directly monetized work.
- LTV or retention impact is material and evidence-backed.
- Strategic, compliance, or platform value justifies short-term ROI trade-off.

Do not build when contribution margin is negative in the downside case or payback exceeds average customer lifetime.

### Acquisition Channel

Scale when:

- LTV:CAC is greater than 3:1.
- Payback is less than 18 months.
- Cohort retention or NRR is comparable to existing channels.
- Channel volume is addressable without quality collapse.

Do not scale when LTV:CAC is below 1.5:1 and no improvement path exists.

### Pricing Change

Proceed only when:

- Net revenue impact remains positive after churn/contraction risk.
- Test segment and rollback plan are defined.
- Customer communication and support impact are understood.

## Red Flags

- Churn increasing cohort-over-cohort.
- NRR below 100%.
- Quick ratio below 2.
- LTV:CAC below 1.5:1.
- Payback above 24 months.
- Gross margin below 60% for SaaS-like businesses.
- Runway below 6 months.
- Rule of 40 below 25 for growth-stage SaaS.
- Top customer above 25% of revenue or top 10 above 50%.
