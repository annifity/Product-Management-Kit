# Competitive Intelligence Quality Checklist

- Decision consumer, category, competitors, alternatives, date, and cadence are explicit.
- Material claims have direct sources and observed or publication dates.
- Product behavior is not inferred from marketing copy alone.
- Fact, inference, and assumption are labeled consistently.
- The current record is diffed against a named previous snapshot when one exists.
- New, changed, contradicted, and unsupported claims are distinguishable.
- Product implications include counterevidence, confidence, validation, and owner.
- Feature parity is rejected unless independently supported by user and strategy evidence.
- Watch triggers have source, cadence, owner, and action.
- No roadmap or external action is changed automatically.
