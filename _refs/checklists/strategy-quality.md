# Strategy Quality Checklist

Use before approving a product strategy or portfolio decision.

## Choice Quality

- [ ] Decision owner and time horizon are explicit.
- [ ] Target users or segments and strategic problem are evidence-linked.
- [ ] Where-to-play and how-to-win choices are explicit.
- [ ] Non-goals, rejected alternatives, and displaced work are visible.
- [ ] The recommendation is not merely a feature list or delivery plan.

## Evidence And Economics

- [ ] Claims are separated into evidence, inference, assumption, and preference.
- [ ] Confidence and material counterevidence are stated.
- [ ] Investment, operating cost, opportunity cost, and capacity constraints are considered.
- [ ] AI suitability, risk tier, evaluation burden, and unit economics are addressed when AI is material.

## Portfolio Coherence

- [ ] Each bet has an explore, validate, invest, sustain, sequence, pause, or stop posture.
- [ ] Dependencies and conflicts across bets are visible.
- [ ] The selected set fits stated constraints.
- [ ] Strategic outcomes, guardrails, invalidation conditions, and review triggers are measurable.

## Governance

- [ ] Decision, dissent, approver, consulted stakeholders, date, and review date are recorded.
- [ ] Affected commitments and downstream artifact owners are named.
- [ ] Delivery dates or scope are not implied without a confirmed spec and plan.
