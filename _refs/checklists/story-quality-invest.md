# Story Quality Checklist

Use INVEST to evaluate the User Story slice. Evaluate criterion-level quality separately with `_refs/checklists/acceptance-criteria-quality.md`.

- Independent: can be delivered without hidden dependency.
- Negotiable: describes value, not rigid implementation.
- Valuable: user or business value is clear.
- Estimable: enough context exists to size it.
- Small: fits inside a sprint slice.
- Testable: the story has acceptance criteria that define objective acceptance.
- Dependencies and open questions are visible.
