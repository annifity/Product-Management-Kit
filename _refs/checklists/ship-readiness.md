# Ship Readiness Checklist

- Scope is confirmed.
- Blocking defects are resolved or accepted.
- UAT signoff exists.
- Requirement traceability is updated.
- REQ-ID -> STORY-ID -> AC-ID -> TC-ID -> RELEASE CHECK gaps are accepted or closed.
- Release note is ready.
- Stakeholders know the change.
- Rollback or support plan exists when needed.
- Operational runbook, alert, and escalation gaps are resolved or accepted.
- Security, privacy, accessibility, and AI risks are resolved or accepted with owner and review date.
- For an AI-enabled release or material model, prompt, retrieval, tool, policy,
  or data change, a versioned baseline-to-candidate evaluation verdict exists
  and every critical slice and hard blocker passes.
- AI evaluation limitations, permitted deployment scope, monitoring, human fallback,
  and rollback signals are included in the release package.
- Launch metrics and owner are identified.
- Docs index is updated.
- Final decisions and lessons are saved to memories.
