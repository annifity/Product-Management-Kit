# GTM Quality Checklist

- Product scope, ICP, buyer, user, desired behavior, horizon, and owner are explicit.
- Positioning names the nearest real alternative and evidence-backed differentiation.
- Launch tier and audience sequence match risk, value, and readiness.
- Each channel has a target segment, rationale, owner, and measurable behavior.
- Messaging does not claim unsupported capability or evidence.
- Enablement covers sales, support, operations, and affected internal teams as applicable.
- Adoption metrics include baseline, target, window, source, guardrails, and stop conditions.
- Pricing decisions route to `commercial`; operational release readiness routes to `ship`.
- The GTM verdict does not claim the product is release-ready.
