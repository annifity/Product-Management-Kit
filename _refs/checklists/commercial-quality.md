# Commercial Quality Checklist

- Decision, alternatives, customer, owner, horizon, and reversibility are explicit.
- Every input has value, unit, period, population, source, date, and confidence.
- Gross/net, user/account, monthly/annual, and revenue/margin measures are not mixed.
- Supported calculations use repository scripts and preserve raw inputs and formulas.
- Base, downside, and upside scenarios expose the assumptions that can reverse the result.
- Customer value, willingness-to-pay, packaging, adoption, revenue, and margin implications are connected.
- Missing denominators produce `Not available`, not an invented benchmark.
- Rejected options, invalidation conditions, verdict, owner, and review trigger are present.
