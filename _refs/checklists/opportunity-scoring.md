# Opportunity Scoring

Use for strategic discovery, roadmap candidate review, or feature-level go/no-go.

Score each dimension from 1 to 5 using evidence, not confidence theater.

| Dimension | Score 1 | Score 3 | Score 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impact | Small local improvement | Meaningful workflow or metric gain | Strategic, revenue, compliance, or major user impact |
| Confidence | Mostly assumption | Some evidence from one or two sources | Evidence from at least three independent signals |
| Strategic Fit | Weak or unclear fit | Supports a known theme | Directly supports an OKR, roadmap theme, or urgent business constraint |
| Commercial Attractiveness | No clear market, pricing, or ROI signal | Plausible business case but assumptions need validation | Market size, pricing, retention, or ROI evidence supports investment |
| Learnability | Build-first with little learning value | Some cheap validation possible | Clear low-cost experiment can reduce major uncertainty |

Decision:

- 20-25: Strong candidate for roadmap or deeper feature discovery.
- 15-19: Explore if capacity exists; clarify evidence gaps first.
- 0-14: Park, revisit later, or reject with rationale.

Always record source evidence and the reason for the decision.

Use `_refs/workflows/market-sizing.md` when Commercial Attractiveness depends on TAM/SAM/SOM, and `_refs/checklists/finance-metrics.md` when ROI, pricing, retention, or channel economics drive the decision.
