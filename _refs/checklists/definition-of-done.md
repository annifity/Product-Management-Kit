# Definition Of Done

Use this when reviewing whether a feature can be accepted, released, or handed off.

- Accepted scope matches baselined requirements and change log.
- All agreed user stories meet acceptance criteria.
- Critical and high defects are resolved or explicitly accepted.
- UAT coverage and signoff are complete.
- Requirement traceability is updated from requirement to story to test.
- Release notes and stakeholder summary are ready.
- Operational runbooks, alerts, and escalation paths are ready when needed.
- Support and rollback plans are documented when user or operational impact is material.
- Docs index, decision log, and durable memories are updated.
- Post-release metric owner and review date are identified.
