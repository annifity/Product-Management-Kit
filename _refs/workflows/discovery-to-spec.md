# Discovery To Spec

1. Capture the raw idea or stakeholder ask.
2. Identify problem, users, outcome, constraints, and assumptions.
3. Explore options and select a recommended direction.
4. Confirm scope boundaries with the user.
5. Convert the confirmed brief into requirements, workflows, data, risks, and open questions.
6. Save the brief and spec through `docs`.
7. Persist durable context through `memories`.
