# Product Growth Workflow

Use this workflow to find a sustainable growth constraint and convert it into an evidence-backed intervention plan.

## 1. Establish The Model

Define the ICP, product type, natural usage frequency, critical value event, acquisition unit, retention unit, revenue unit, and growth horizon. Distinguish user, account, logo, and revenue behavior.

## 2. Map The System

Map acquisition quality, activation/time-to-value, engagement, retention, referral, expansion, and monetization. For each stage record volume, rate, denominator, time window, segment, source, and downstream consequence.

Do not treat the funnel as linear when a loop exists. Record the loop input, value event, output, reinvestment mechanism, cycle time, and break point.

## 3. Find The Constraint

Prioritize the stage with the largest decision-relevant loss, not the most visible percentage. Separate:

- Wrong-user or wrong-channel acquisition
- Activation failure
- Weak recurring value or natural frequency
- Product-quality or reliability failure
- Collaboration, data, workflow, or network depth
- Pricing or packaging mismatch
- Missing distribution or enablement

## 4. Diagnose Drivers

Compare cohorts and segments. For a behavior associated with retention or expansion, record correlation size, addressable population, plausible mechanism, confounders, and the test needed before calling it causal.

## 5. Design The Plan

Choose two or three interventions against the binding constraint. For each define target population, behavior change, expected impact range, guardrail, minimum test, owner, cost, and review window.

Reject generic tactics such as “improve engagement,” notification volume without recurring value, reactivation before activation is fixed, or growth spend that hides poor retention.

## Verdict

Use `Proceed`, `Proceed with conditions`, `Need evidence`, `Reframe`, or `Do not proceed`. Send measurement gaps to `analytics`, tests to `experiment`, market motion to `gtm`, and pricing/packaging to `commercial`.
