# Competitive Intelligence Workflow

Use this workflow to create a sourced baseline or update a recurring competitor watch without turning marketing claims into roadmap commitments.

## 1. Scope

Define the decision consumer, category boundary, competitor set, alternatives/status quo, source policy, previous snapshot, observation date, and monitoring cadence. Exclude companies that are not comparable and explain why.

## 2. Evidence Collection

Prefer primary sources: product documentation, pricing pages, release notes, filings, official announcements, job postings, and direct product observation. Use secondary sources to triangulate, not as sole proof of product behavior.

Record URL, title, publisher, publication or observed date, retrieved date, claim supported, and confidence. Time-sensitive claims without dates are incomplete.

## 3. Classification

Label each item:

- **Fact:** directly supported by the cited source.
- **Inference:** reasoned implication from facts, with the reasoning shown.
- **Assumption:** plausible but unverified; never use as battlecard fact.

## 4. Diff

Compare the current record with the prior snapshot. Classify claims as new, changed, unchanged, contradicted, or no longer supported. Avoid treating absence of an announcement as evidence that nothing changed.

## 5. Product Implication

For each material signal state affected customer/job, potential implication, counterevidence, confidence, earliest validation, and decision owner. Reject feature parity unless it supports an independently validated user and strategy choice.

## 6. Watch Plan

Define trigger, source, cadence, owner, and action if triggered. The output informs `strategy`, `gtm`, `commercial`, or `discovery`; it never edits the roadmap automatically.
