# Learning Synthesis

Use after prototype review, experiment validation, UAT, release, or post-ship review.

1. Collect the original hypothesis, criteria, evidence, and result notes.
   When the input is assessed customer-discovery evidence, retain the source,
   finding, counterevidence, privacy, and confidence links established by
   `_refs/workflows/customer-discovery-synthesis.md`.
2. Separate observations from interpretation.
3. Compare results against success metrics and guardrails.
4. Identify what changed in user understanding, product direction, risk, or roadmap confidence.
5. Decide: iterate, specify, plan, ship, park, or reject.
6. Capture reusable insight in `docs` and durable context in `memories`.
7. Recommend the next loop or delivery skill.

## Quality Bar

- Evidence is traceable.
- Confidence level is explicit.
- Decision owner is named.
- Open questions have owners.
- Roadmap recommendation is actionable.
- Customer insights remain traceable to assessed findings and source IDs.
