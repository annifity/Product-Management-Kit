# Solution Exploration Workflow

Use when the problem is understood but the team is anchored on one solution too early.

## Process

1. Frame the problem in one sentence without solution language.
2. List constraints and known facts separately from assumptions.
3. Diverge: generate at least five distinct options before evaluating.
4. Include at least one removal option, one inversion option, and one analogy option.
5. Provoke: stress-test the top options and name the riskiest assumption for each.
6. Converge: compare top options on user value, business impact, complexity, operational cost, risk, and time to ship.
7. If the team needs outcome-to-opportunity structure, use `_refs/templates/strategy/opportunity-solution-tree.md`.
8. Recommend one direction with explicit trade-offs and validation steps.

## Output

- Problem frame.
- Option profiles.
- Assumption map.
- Comparison matrix.
- Recommended option.
- Top assumptions to validate before build.
- Experiment or proof-of-life test for the riskiest assumption.

Do not evaluate during divergence. Constraints apply during convergence.
