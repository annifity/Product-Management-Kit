# Release Readiness

1. Confirm scope in release.
2. Verify PRD/spec baseline.
3. Verify user stories and acceptance criteria.
4. Verify UAT coverage and signoff.
5. Check open risks and accepted exceptions.
6. Verify operational readiness: support, escalation, alert, rollback, and known limitations.
7. Prepare rollout plan, release note, and stakeholder summary.
8. Identify launch metrics, owner, and post-launch review date.
9. Save final docs and memories.
