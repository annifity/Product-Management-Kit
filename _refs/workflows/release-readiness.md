# Release Readiness

1. Confirm scope in release.
2. Verify PRD/spec baseline.
3. Verify user stories and acceptance criteria.
4. Verify UAT coverage and signoff.
5. Check open risks and accepted exceptions.
6. Review security, privacy, accessibility, and AI risk using `_refs/checklists/security-privacy-accessibility.md` when relevant.
7. Verify operational readiness: support, escalation, alert, rollback, and known limitations.
8. Name decision maker, approver, accepted-risk owner, and informed stakeholders using `_refs/checklists/stakeholder-governance.md`.
9. Prepare rollout plan, release note, and stakeholder summary.
10. Identify launch metrics, owner, and post-launch review date.
11. Save final docs and memories.
