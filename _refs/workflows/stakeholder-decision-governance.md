# Stakeholder Decision Governance Workflow

Use when a material product choice needs clear authority, consultation, dissent handling, escalation, and durable communication.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision, scope, deadline, reversibility, and cost of delay.
2. Name the decision owner, approver when different, consulted stakeholders, informed stakeholders, and tie-breaker.
3. Document alternatives, evidence, assumptions, trade-offs, affected commitments, and residual risks.
4. Separate factual disagreement, value or priority disagreement, and authority conflict.
5. Resolve missing evidence through the owning workflow; do not use consensus to manufacture facts.
6. Record dissent and the conditions that would reopen the decision.
7. Capture the decision, rationale, owner, accepted risks, date, review date, and communication plan.
8. Verify that downstream artifacts and teams received the decision without silently changing the accepted baseline.

## Escalation Rules

- Escalate when the owner is absent, authority overlaps, the deadline will be missed, or a material risk lacks an accepter.
- Route committed product-scope movement to `change`.
- Route day-to-day implementation clarification to `execution`.
- Require explicit approval before external publication, bulk mutation, release, or other consequential action.
