# Research Evidence Workflow

Use when discovery, market analysis, company research, competitor analysis, or strategic recommendations require source-backed evidence.

## Source Rules

- Prefer primary sources: official company pages, product docs, filings, earnings calls, pricing pages, release notes, regulatory sources, public datasets.
- Use secondary sources only when primary sources are unavailable or to triangulate market context.
- Capture date, source type, URL or local path, and the exact claim supported.
- Separate evidence, inference, assumption, and opinion.
- Mark stale evidence when source date is older than the decision horizon.
- Do not present uncited external claims as facts.

## Evidence Quality

| Quality | Criteria | Use |
|---|---|---|
| High | Primary source, recent, directly supports claim | Can support roadmap/business decision. |
| Medium | Credible secondary source or dated primary source | Use with caveat or triangulation. |
| Low | Anecdote, unsourced quote, old market estimate, unclear methodology | Treat as signal, not proof. |

## Research Process

1. Define research objective and decision to support.
2. List key questions and decision criteria.
3. Gather evidence from at least three independent signals when the decision is material.
4. Summarize each source into claim, signal, limitation, and confidence.
5. Identify conflicts across sources.
6. Convert evidence into implications for product, market, risk, or roadmap.
7. Record open questions and next validation step.

## Output

| Claim | Evidence Source | Quality | Confidence | Limitation | Product Implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Claim] | [URL/path + date] | High / Medium / Low | High / Medium / Low | [Caveat] | [Decision impact] |

## Company Research Brief Sections

- Company overview.
- Product strategy and recent launches.
- Executive or official source signals.
- Customer/segment signals.
- GTM and pricing signals.
- AI/digital transformation signals when relevant.
- Competitive implications.
- Unknowns and validation plan.
