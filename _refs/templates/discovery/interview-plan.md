## Interview Framework

### Opening

- Thank participant.
- Explain this is research, not a sales pitch.
- Ask permission to take notes or record.

### Core Questions

| Question | Rationale | Follow-up | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tell me about the last time you [experienced problem]. | Past behavior, not hypotheticals. | What did you try? What happened next? | Would you use our product? |
| How do you currently handle [problem]? | Workaround and pain intensity. | How much time/money does that take? | Isn't that inefficient? |
| What alternatives have you tried? | Competitive and substitute behavior. | Why did you stop or continue? | Would you switch if... |

### Closing

- Summarize what you heard and ask for correction.
- Ask for referrals when appropriate.
- Thank the participant.

## Biases To Avoid

- Confirmation bias.
- Leading questions.
- Hypothetical willingness-to-pay questions.
- Pitching during discovery.
- Stopping after one or two confirming interviews.

## Success Criteria

- Specific stories, not generic complaints.
- Past behavior, not wishes.
- Patterns across at least three independent interviews.
- Verbatim customer language captured.
- Surprising insight or invalidated assumption recorded.
