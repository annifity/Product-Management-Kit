# Milestones

Use for a delivery milestone schedule tied to committed scope, not aspirational dates.

## Milestone Schedule

| Milestone | Target Date | Exit Criteria | Owner | Dependencies | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Milestone] | [Date] | [Objectively checkable criteria] | [Owner] | [Deps] | Planned / At Risk / Committed / Done |

## Checklist

- Every milestone has exit criteria that are objectively checkable, not a date alone.
- Every milestone has one accountable owner.
- Slippage risk and mitigation are recorded before the date is communicated externally.
- Each milestone links back to the release slice or epic it closes.
