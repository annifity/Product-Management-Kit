# Positioning Statement

Use for a single, testable positioning claim that anchors messaging, differentiation, and target-segment framing for a strategic choice.

## Statement

- For: [target customer or segment]
- Who: [statement of need or opportunity]
- The [product or initiative name] is a: [product category]
- That: [key benefit or compelling reason to prefer]
- Unlike: [primary competitive alternative or status quo]
- Our product/initiative: [primary differentiation]

## Validation
