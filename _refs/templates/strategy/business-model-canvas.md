# Business Model Canvas

Use for strategy, new product, or business model exploration.

| Block | Questions |
|---|---|
| Customer Segments | Who are users, buyers, and priority segments? |
| Value Propositions | What problem is solved and what makes the solution different? |
| Channels | How do users discover, evaluate, buy, receive, and get support? |
| Customer Relationships | What relationship does each segment expect? |
| Revenue Streams | How does value convert into revenue or business benefit? |
| Key Resources | What people, systems, data, assets, or capital are required? |
| Key Activities | What must the business do to deliver the value proposition? |
| Key Partners | Which partners or suppliers are essential? |
| Cost Structure | What fixed, variable, and scaling costs matter? |

## Completion Checklist

- Value proposition is measurable.
- Customer segments are specific.
- Revenue or business benefit is linked to segments.
- Key resources support key activities.
- Cost structure matches delivery model.
- Assumptions and risks are explicit.
- Linked to roadmap, OKRs, discovery, or research when available.
