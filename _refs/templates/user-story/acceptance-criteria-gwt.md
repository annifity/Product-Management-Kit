# Given When Then Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: [Behavior-focused scenario name] (AC-01)
  Given [material actor, state, permission, or data precondition]
  When [single primary action or event]
  Then [observable business or system-boundary outcome]
  And [additional result needed to prove the same coherent behavior]
```

Apply `_refs/checklists/acceptance-criteria-quality.md` whether the output uses Given/When/Then or another user-requested format. Keep AC IDs stable and map them to source requirements or rules through the artifact or RTM.
