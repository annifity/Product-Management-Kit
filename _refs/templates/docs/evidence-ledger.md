## Rules

- Every external factual claim needs a source or must be labeled as an assumption.
- Prefer primary sources for market, competitor, pricing, policy, and product claims.
- If sources conflict, record the conflict instead of averaging them away.
- Mark stale evidence when the source is older than the decision horizon.
- Link material evidence to PRD/spec/roadmap decisions and risk register rows.
