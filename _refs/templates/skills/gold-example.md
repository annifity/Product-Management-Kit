# Gold Example Contract

Use this structure to author a synthetic quality anchor for one material artifact family.

## Scenario

- Product and user context
- Decision and audience
- Evidence supplied
- Deliberately missing evidence

## Gold Output

Provide one complete artifact using the canonical template. Include an explicit non-goal, counterevidence or rejected option, labeled assumptions, a decision verdict, and a next action.

## Why This Is Gold

| Quality dimension | Observable evidence in the example |
|---|---|
| Decision usefulness | [What decision becomes possible] |
| Evidence discipline | [How fact, inference, and assumption are separated] |
| Scope restraint | [What is explicitly excluded] |
| Testability | [How metrics or acceptance signals can be checked] |
| Handoff quality | [Owner, next action, and unresolved blocker] |

## Anti-Pattern Contrast

Show one short failing excerpt and explain the decision harm it would create. Do not include generic filler or copied external examples.
